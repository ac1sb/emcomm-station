Which band when (rule of thumb)
Nearby (0-300 miles, NVIS):  80 m at night, 40 m by day, 60 m either
Regional (300-1000 miles):   40 m, 30 m digital, 20 m by day
Long distance:               20 m by day, 40 m at night, 17/15/10 m near solar maximum
